# pm-growth-coach

**TL;DR & So What:** A self-development coach for product managers — one skill that baselines you against a five-dimension PM competency rubric (structured discovery, systems thinking, strategic narrative, execution judgment, stakeholder management), helps you pick the one or two capabilities with the most leverage for your goal, and builds a growth plan of small weekly practice reps attached to your real work. If you use a product workflow suite (like product-flow), it reads your actual artifacts as evidence — "your last three specs all skipped non-goals" — instead of relying on self-report.

So what: this develops the PM, where a workflow suite develops the product. It scores only with evidence, checks the halo effect when you're transitioning from an adjacent role, requires new evidence (not elapsed time) to move a score, and treats stalled progress as a plan problem rather than a person problem. Your growth data stays in a private `pm-growth/` folder you own.

## What's inside

One skill (`pm-growth-coach`) with two references: the competency rubric (behavioral anchors at developing/solid/advanced levels, plus workspace-evidence pointers per dimension) and the growth-plan + practice-journal templates (deliberate-practice reps, re-scoring rules). `evals/evals.json` carries post-install test prompts, including boundary checks.

## Boundaries (by design)

- Doing product work (PRDs, roadmaps, discovery) → a product workflow suite, not this.
- Getting feedback on a specific document → a critique skill; bring the *pattern* back here as evidence.
- Coaching someone else as their coach (assessments, sponsor memos) → a separate coach-side kit; this skill is self-development only.

## The product line

product-flow does the work · **pm-growth-coach develops the PM** · a coach kit serves the professional coach. Three users, three tools, no trigger overlap.
