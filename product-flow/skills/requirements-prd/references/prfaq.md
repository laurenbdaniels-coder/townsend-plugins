# PR-FAQ (Working Backwards) — Template

For "should we build this at all?" on new products and major initiatives. **Two-pass rule:** pass 1 is an explicitly labeled hypothesis draft — assumptions tagged, testimonials marked `[ASPIRATIONAL — replace with real customer voice before stakeholder review]`. Pass 2 replaces tags with discovery evidence before any leadership or investor reading. Never present pass 1 as validated. Truth-seeking over selling: the internal FAQ exists to make the case *against* as sharp as the case for.

---

## TL;DR & So What

*Max 2 paragraphs: (1) the thing, the customer, and the claim, (2) pass 1 or pass 2, what must be true, and the decision this doc serves.*

## Press release (≤1 page, dated at launch, plain customer language)

- **Headline** — *what a customer would care about, not what the company is proud of.*
- **Problem paragraph** — *from the customer's view: the situation, what it costs them (show, don't tell — steps, hours, dollars), and why current alternatives fall short. Every factual claim sourced or tagged.*
- **Solution paragraphs** — *what it is, how a customer starts, why it resolves the problem — acknowledging the alternatives honestly.*
- **Customer quote** — *pass 1: `[ASPIRATIONAL]` label mandatory. Pass 2: a real quote with a source line.*
- **Internal quote** — *why we built it, in one honest sentence.*
- **How to get started.**

## External FAQ

*What customers/press would ask: price, availability, compatibility, migration, privacy. TK what's undecided — never invent.*

## Internal FAQ (where the honest work happens)

- How big is the opportunity — bottom-up, inputs sourced or `[ASSUMPTION]`-tagged?
- **Why this, why us, why now** — answer as Insight → Data → Implication triplets (see strategy-vision's why-case): each a falsifiable insight, the sourced evidence behind it, and the implication that points at this product now. If the why-case exists in `30-strategy-onepager.md`, inherit it — don't reinvent it.
- What's the technical risk, and what did the feasibility read say?
- How do the unit economics work, even roughly?
- **What must be true for this to succeed** — the load-bearing assumptions, each with confirm/kill evidence?
- **The strongest case against building it** — steelmanned, not strawmanned?
- What's the cheapest test before commitment?

## Review protocol

*Readers read silently, then discuss. Collect the strongest objections into the internal FAQ — a PR-FAQ improves by absorbing attacks, not deflecting them. For a formal pass, run artifact-critique (pre-mortem mode pairs well). Gate rule: pass 2 headed to leadership surfaces any remaining `[ASSUMPTION]`s in an open-assumptions section — tag-and-warn, never silently.*

## Quality bar

- [ ] Pass 1 vs. pass 2 status labeled at the top
- [ ] Headline and problem written from the customer's world, jargon-free
- [ ] All testimonials real-and-sourced or `[ASPIRATIONAL]`-labeled — no exceptions
- [ ] Alternatives acknowledged honestly (including "do nothing")
- [ ] Internal FAQ contains the steelmanned case against
- [ ] Why-this/why-us/why-now answered as insight → data → implication, not conviction
- [ ] "What must be true" list has confirm/kill evidence per item
- [ ] No unresolved load-bearing assumptions in a pass-2 leadership doc without an open-assumptions section
