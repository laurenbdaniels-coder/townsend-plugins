# Customer Interview Guide — Template

Mom Test discipline (Fitzpatrick) with JTBD switch/forces framing (Christensen/Moesta). Three rules govern every question: talk about their life, not your idea; ask about specific past events, never hypotheticals; listen ~80%, talk ~20%. Compliments are not data — past behavior and money/time spent are.

---

## Recruiting

*5–8 people who recently faced the problem (last 90 days beats last year). For switching insight, prioritize people who recently adopted or abandoned a solution — theirs, a competitor's, or a workaround. Screen for the target segment, not for friendliness.*

## Question bank (adapt 6–10; past tense throughout)

**Opening the story:**
- "Walk me through the last time [problem situation] happened."
- "What were you doing right before? What triggered dealing with it that day?"

**The job and the forces (JTBD):**
- "What were you trying to get done, bigger-picture?" *(the job, not the task)*
- "What pushed you to look for something new?" *(push)* · "What drew you to [what they chose]?" *(pull)*
- "What almost stopped you from switching?" *(anxiety)* · "What did you like about the old way?" *(habit)*

**Cost and workarounds (where the evidence lives):**
- "What did you do about it? Show me if you can."
- "What else did you try? What happened?"
- "What did that cost you — time, money, favors?"
- "Who else was involved or affected?"

**Closing:**
- "What haven't I asked about that mattered?"
- "Who else deals with this that I should talk to?"

## Banned questions

*"Would you use…?" · "Would you pay for…?" · "How much would you pay?" · "Do you think it's a good idea?" — hypotheticals produce polite fiction. If pricing signal is needed, anchor on observed spend and use range reactions AFTER pain discussion (see market-positioning's viability check).*

## No-pitching rule

*Don't describe the product until the interview is effectively over. The moment you pitch, every answer becomes a favor.*

## Note capture (per interview, within 48h)

| Field | Capture |
|---|---|
| Facts | verifiable statements about their situation |
| Quotes | verbatim, with role + context |
| Workarounds | what they actually do today |
| Cost paid | time/money/effort already spent on the problem |
| Forces | push / pull / anxiety / habit observed |
| Surprises & disconfirmations | anything that cuts against the hypothesis |

*Interpretations stay out of the capture table — they belong in the synthesis readout (references/synthesis-readout.md), where every claim gets a source and a confidence rating.*

## Quality bar

- [ ] Every question is about a specific past event, in past tense
- [ ] No banned questions anywhere in the guide
- [ ] Recruiting screen matches the target segment
- [ ] Capture template distinguishes facts/quotes from interpretation
- [ ] Notes logged within 48 hours; insights filed to `85-insight-log.md`
