# Research Synthesis Readout — Template

Produced when interview/research data comes back. The structure that makes synthesis trustworthy: claim → source → confidence, with the disconfirming section mandatory. Prioritize needs by evidence weight, not by loudest voice. Evidence rules (`standards/evidence-rules.md`) bind every cell.

---

## TL;DR & So What

*Max 2 paragraphs: (1) what the evidence says — the strongest finding with its count (e.g., "6 of 8 raised X unprompted"), and the strongest disconfirming signal, (2) the verdict against the pre-registered thresholds and the next move.*

## 1. Decision context

*Restate the decision this research informs and the pre-registered GO/NO-GO thresholds — verbatim, from before fieldwork. The readout answers those criteria; it does not invent new ones after seeing the data. If no thresholds were pre-registered, set them now and label them post-hoc.*

## 2. Prioritized needs table

*One row per need/pain, ordered by evidence weight. Confidence: High = multiple independent sources; Medium = single good source; Low = indirect, dated, or uncited. Flag sources older than ~12 months.*

| Need / pain | Verbatim evidence (quote + source + date) | Business consequence | Current workaround & what it costs them | Who buys / who blocks | Urgency | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TK | "…" (Source: role/segment, date, interview) | TK | TK | TK | TK | High/Med/Low |

## 3. Switching triggers

*The events that pushed people to act — "final straw" moments from the forces interviews. Triggers are what marketing and sales can actually watch for. Each with its source line.*

## 4. What we looked for and didn't find

*Mandatory. Evidence you actively sought that would have supported (or killed) each hypothesis and did NOT appear: pains nobody raised unprompted, spend that wasn't there, urgency that didn't materialize. Plus: what would change these conclusions. A readout without this section is advocacy.*

## 5. Open assumptions

*Every `[ASSUMPTION]` still standing after fieldwork, each with confirm/kill evidence and an owner. Don't let assumptions ride into positioning or the PRD unlabeled.*

## 6. Verdict against thresholds

*GO threshold met / NO-GO threshold met / inconclusive — plainly. Feeds the problem statement, positioning, and (if choosing between directions) the wedge scorer.*

## 7. File the insights

*Each durable finding becomes an INSIGHT-NNN entry in `85-insight-log.md`: claim (one sentence, falsifiable, solution-free), source line, confidence, implications, status. Log within 48 hours — after that, memory rewrites the data. Lost deals and misses file too; that's where disconfirming evidence lives.*

## Quality bar

- [ ] Opens with the pre-registered thresholds, unedited (or labeled post-hoc)
- [ ] Every claim has a source line, `[ASSUMPTION]` tag, or TK — no naked claims
- [ ] Confidence follows the definitions, not gut feel; counts stated (n)
- [ ] "Looked for and didn't find" present and substantive
- [ ] No compliments counted as evidence; past behavior and money/time spent only
- [ ] Insights filed to `85-insight-log.md` within 48h
