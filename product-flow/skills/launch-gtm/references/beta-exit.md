# Beta Program & Exit Criteria — Template

Use when validation routing (`standards/decision-frameworks.md`) says beta: usage-and-fit risk, operational-burden risk, or a one-way-door launch. The load-bearing rule: **exit criteria are written before the beta starts.** A beta without pre-committed exit criteria is a soft launch wearing a beta badge — it can't end, and it can't say no.

---

## TL;DR & So What

*Max 2 paragraphs: (1) why this needs a beta (the risk it mitigates) and what "graduate to GA" requires, (2) current read and the next decision date.*

## 1. Justification

*Which risk does this beta mitigate that internal testing can't? (Live-usage fit, operational load, one-way-door caution.) If the honest answer is "none," skip the beta — route to staged dial-up instead.*

## 2. Exit criteria (pre-committed)

*What data, what threshold, by when = GA. Include the disconfirming threshold: what result means "do not GA as-is."*

## 3. The three non-success branches (decide the policy now)

*If the data misses the bar: **improve** (named fixes, another cycle with a cap on cycles), **hold** (park with a revisit date), or **sunset** (wind down with participant comms). Choosing the branch after the data arrives invites drift toward "one more cycle" forever.*

## 4. Participants

*Selection criteria matched to the positioning doc's best-fit segment — not just friendly logos. Entry expectations (usage commitment, feedback cadence), what they get (access, influence, pricing consideration), and the feedback channel (routes to `70-launch-learnings.md`, durable insights to `85-insight-log.md`).*

## 5. Timeline and comms

*Start, checkpoints, decision date. What participants are told at entry about how and when the beta ends — exits surprise people only when nobody wrote this down.*

## Quality bar

- [ ] Beta justified by a named risk internal testing can't cover
- [ ] Exit criteria (including the blocking threshold) pre-committed before start
- [ ] Non-success branches decided in advance, with a cycle cap
- [ ] Participants match the target segment; entry expectations written
- [ ] Feedback routes to the learnings file and insight log, win or lose
- [ ] Decision date on the calendar with an owner
