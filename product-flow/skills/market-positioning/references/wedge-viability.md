# Wedge Scorer & Viability Check — Templates

Two artifacts for choosing where to enter and confirming the business can win there. The wedge scorer picks ONE beachhead (Moore's Crossing the Chasm discipline: dominate a segment you can win outright before expanding). The viability check tests Cagan's fourth risk — does this work for OUR business, not just the customer? Both are signal-level reads, not forecasts — say so on page one.

---

## Part 1: Wedge scorer

## TL;DR & So What

*Max 2 paragraphs: (1) which wedge won and the decisive criteria, (2) what's still assumed and the review date that would reopen the pick.*

### Candidates

*2–4 candidate wedges (segment × use case). Note which was "preferred" going in — scrutinize that one hardest.*

### Scoring matrix

*Score 1–5 per criterion per wedge. Every score cites its evidence (insight-log ID or source line) + confidence — **an uncited score drops to Low confidence automatically, whatever the number says.** Don't weight-and-sum past a fatal weakness: a 1 on pain acuity or reachability is usually disqualifying regardless of total.*

| Criterion | What 5 looks like | Wedge A: score / evidence / conf | Wedge B: … |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pain acuity | Pain raised unprompted by multiple accounts, with business consequences and active spend on workarounds | TK | TK |
| Willingness to pay | Real spend observed or credible pricing reactions — not "they said they'd pay" | TK | TK |
| Reachability / channel fit | A known, affordable path to this segment exists | TK | TK |
| Competitive intensity | Few credible alternatives; incumbents weak on the job that matters | TK | TK |
| Differentiator decisiveness | Our edge is the reason buyers would *decide* — backed by decision-level evidence (a switch, a lost deal, a purchase), not preference | TK | TK |
| Expansion potential | Winning this wedge opens adjacent segments | TK | TK |
| Delivery feasibility | We can serve this wedge well at realistic scope | TK | TK |

### Disconfirming check & selection

*Restate what was pre-registered as disconfirming for the leading wedge and whether it appeared (pull from the synthesis readout's "looked for and didn't find"). If it appeared and is being overridden, say so and why. Then: selected wedge + rationale (name the decisive criteria), runner-up and why not, riskiest-assumption result (confirmed / disconfirmed / inconclusive — cite it), and **what specific future evidence would change the pick, with a review date.***

## Part 2: Viability check

## TL;DR & So What

*Max 2 paragraphs: (1) the verdict — viable signal / concerns / non-viable signal — and the read that drives it, (2) what would change the verdict and when it gets rechecked.*

### Pricing reaction

*Anchor to observed spend on workarounds and alternatives (from the teardown). Range questions ("at what price would this feel expensive but worth it / out of the question?") asked AFTER pain discussion, never "would you pay $X?" Weight what they already pay far above what they say they'd pay. Small n → label signal-level.*

### Can it be sold? (channel reality test)

*Team with sales: put a one-paragraph pitch + straw-man price in front of sellers who carry a number — "could you sell this tomorrow, to whom specifically, what's the first objection?" Sellers dodging "who, specifically" is a red flag. Solo founder / self-serve: pitch it through your actual channel (landing page, cold outreach, community post) and record verbatim reactions and conversion signal. Read: sellable / sellable-with-conditions / not sellable as framed.*

### Channel economics

*Does the buying motion this product needs match channels you have or can afford? A channel that costs more per sale than the deal returns is a NO signal regardless of demand.*

### Complement or cannibalize?

*Does this pull revenue/attention from existing offers or feed them? Tag magnitude estimates `[ASSUMPTION]`. Cannibalization is a one-way-door signal — flag for full gate treatment per `standards/decision-frameworks.md`.*

### Legal / compliance flags

*Name anything needing review before commitment (regulated data, claims marketing couldn't substantiate, contractual promises); assign an owner; don't resolve here and don't silently assume clearance.*

### Verdict

*One of three — viable signal / concerns (with named conditions, owners, clearing evidence) / non-viable signal — with the driving evidence. No unresolved `[ASSUMPTION]`s on load-bearing claims here; surface them in an open-assumptions list instead.*

## Quality bar

- [ ] One wedge selected — not a portfolio of maybes; runner-up documented
- [ ] Every score cited or auto-dropped to Low; fatal-weakness rule applied
- [ ] Differentiator decisiveness backed by decision-level evidence, not compliments
- [ ] Pricing anchored to observed spend; nothing presented as a forecast
- [ ] Sell-test run through the real channel, verbatim reactions captured
- [ ] Cannibalization called, with door flag if warranted
- [ ] "What would change the pick/verdict" named, with a review date
